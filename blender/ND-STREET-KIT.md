# ND street kit

Rebuilt September 2026 on `testing`. These are original, generic street
accessories fitted to the existing ND asset, not replicas of branded products.
The base car files are unchanged. Their imported meshes are used only for
measuring and surface queries; no donor topology is copied into these parts.

| Asset | App option | Construction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FA01 | Street Front Lip | Full-width blade, 9 mm section, smooth outer rail and underside fitted to the lower bumper and trim |
| RA06 | Slim Side Extensions | Two closed blades, tapered at both ends, following the lower sill; satin black |
| RA06B | Carbon Extenders | Same fitted side geometry, embedded colour and normal weave maps |
| RA01 | Low Boot Spoiler | 920 mm span, 57 mm central depth, maximum 34 mm rise; painted moulding with fitted underside |
| RA05 | Carbon Lip Spoiler | 920 mm span, 43 mm central depth, maximum 20 mm rise; carbon variant |

Those are authoring dimensions for this visualisation, not manufacturing or
real-world fitment specifications. A nominal 0.7 mm mounting clearance avoids
coplanar surfaces. The boot-edge mouldings taper in depth and height at the ends
and stay behind the raised detail in the donor's centre deck. The former deep
skirts and jagged lifted boot strips are no longer generated by their scripts.

## Rebuild

From the repository root, using Blender 5.2:

```powershell
& 'C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe' --background --factory-startup --python blender/build_nd_street.py -- --render
```

`build_nd_street.py` imports the original ND into a separate background process,
verifies its measured frame, builds all five variants, checks closed topology
and positive volume, then exports the existing catalogue filenames. It also
writes `blender/build/nd_street_report.json`. With `--render`, it saves front,
rear, top, detail and profile inspection views alongside the report.

The shape implementation lives in `blender/nd_street_kit.py`. The original
`blender/mods/FA01_front_lip.py`, `RA06_side_skirts.py` and
`RA05_boot_spoilers.py` entry points now call that implementation, so rebuilding
through an old entry point cannot silently restore the stretched donor panels.

If design dimensions change, update the measured `bboxMm`, materials and budget
for the affected entries in `src/data/modsData.json`, then validate each asset:

```powershell
node scripts/validate-mod.mjs FA01
node scripts/validate-mod.mjs RA06
node scripts/validate-mod.mjs RA06B
node scripts/validate-mod.mjs RA01
node scripts/validate-mod.mjs RA05
```

The final geometry totals are 3,476 triangles for FA01, 4,032 for each skirt
pair, and 1,940 for each spoiler. All five files remain below 260 kB each.
Carbon textures are generated deterministically and embedded in the GLBs.
Materials and textures are disposed by the mod loader when its templates are
released. The validator may report an optional material extension notice;
the application's glTF loader supports the exported clearcoat/specular data.

## Review in the app

Choose **Presets → ND Street Kit**. The preset uses the stock wheel design,
satin lower blades, painted spoiler and a mild 20 mm drop.

**Compare original aero** temporarily removes the front lip, side extensions
and rear spoiler. It preserves camera, lighting, paint, wheel setup and other
selected parts. **Return to your build** restores them. Comparison does not
alter the share URL or undo history. Changing any of those three part choices
or the generation ends comparison automatically.

Render statistics and roof-island inspection controls remain available in the
development server and are omitted from production builds.

Existing unrelated limitations remain: RF geometry, wheel/brake positioning,
performance estimates and other prototype mods were not rebuilt in this pass.
